# Durgam Soft — Content Plan & Placeholder Inventory

This document defines the content guidelines and lists placeholders used across the homepage structure.

---

## 1. Content Rules

To maintain absolute integrity during the architectural phase:

1. **No Invented Statistics**: Never generate metrics, years of experience, or client counts.
2. **No Fake Testimonials**: Testimonial carousels and fabricated reviews are strictly prohibited.
3. **No Fabricated Executive Info**: Founder biography and quotes are kept as placeholders until provided by leadership.
4. **No Speculative Pricing**: Product pricing, subscription tiers, and hardware costs are not invented.
5. **No Fake Contact Details**: Phone numbers, emails, addresses, and social handles are represented as placeholders.
